# V51 Legacy-only visual probe — operator checklist

This is a private local probe, not a deployment candidate and not an engine fix.

1. Serve the repository locally, then open `prototype/legacy_v51_visual_probe/index.html`.
2. Start with a seed, let the hidden clock advance, and confirm the rhythm: a recorded-event template commentary line, a short actual-frame scene, then continuation of that same match. Do not judge only a good-looking scene; the probe adds no quality/result filter.
3. When an NPC goal/save/block has an actor in the existing event receipt, confirm it appears through that independent recorded-event path, not only inside a protagonist scene.
4. For each choice scene, inspect the past-only lead-in (up to five seconds; an early-match scene can have less): formation rails, ball-chasing, late marking, frozen actors, role side, overlap/collision, and whether the bad behaviour is actually visible.
5. Choose one displayed button deliberately. Confirm the label contains the exact displayed `choiceId + targetId`, then watch the same object's actual continuation through scene end.
6. Wait for a later scene. Confirm its clock advances, its scene number changes, and the previous score/state carries on; do not restart between scenes.
7. Try at least two seeds. Record both a bad natural scene and an ordinary scene if they occur. A scene being unpleasant is evidence, not a reason to skip it.
8. Stop and report if commentary appears before its event time, a choice executes before a click, the selected pair differs from the button, a lead-in shows future frames, the match resets between scenes, or a later scene cannot re-enter.

The observation decision is only: whether this Legacy-only presentation makes the remaining natural-scene problems assessable while hidden time is acceptable. It does not approve Legacy, repair movement, or select A as a fallback.
